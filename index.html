About student privacy and publishing

This website works offline for built-in practice after you open the file. Progress and class links stay in this browser using local storage. No student accounts, names, or grades are collected or transmitted by this website. External links may have their own privacy policies. To share the website across devices, upload this HTML file to a static website host approved by your school. Changes made in Teacher Tools do not automatically sync to student devices.
